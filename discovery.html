Our Story

One early morning in the hills above La Libertad, our founder sat watching a pair of urracas hop between branches — intelligent, playful, and unapologetically themselves.

That moment became the seed for Cafe Urraca: a place where people could slow down, be curious, and collect small beautiful moments the way the urraca collects shiny treasures.

THE NAME
Urraca (oo-RAH-kah)
The national bird of intelligence and beauty in many Central American cultures. Known for problem-solving and an eye for the extraordinary.
